Category theory
[
edit
]
Although the Cartesian product is traditionally applied to sets,
category theory
provides a more general interpretation of the
product
of mathematical structures. Product is the simplest example of categorical limit, where the indexing category is discrete. As category of sets can be identified with discrete categories and embedded this way as full subcategory of
Cat
the diagrams indexing products can be reduced to indexing sets matching the set-theoretic definition.
